# Agent guide for this game

This is a [sapling2d](../../packages/engine/llms.txt) game. **Read `node_modules/sapling2d/llms.txt` (or `packages/engine/llms.txt` in the sapling2d repo) before changing code.**

## Layout

英雄守卫 (Hero Guard): a portrait (750×1334) roguelite tower defense. Three unique heroes (archer / mage / knight) move by themselves inside role zones (melee upper middle, ranged lower left / lower right) and attack automatically while enemies walk down random curves; ground enemies leave their path to fight nearby heroes, heroes have HP and respawn from a tombstone; XP level-ups offer one of three skill-tree nodes. Spec, numbers and tickets: `.scratch/hero-guard/` at the repo root. The gray-box prototype it grew from is `examples/towerdefense`.

- `src/game.ts` — `gameOptions` (entry scene `Battle`, action `confirm` = Space / Enter to restart from the result screen; on phones the result screen's button) and the storage keys (`bestWave`, `wins`).
- `src/config.ts` — field, hero zones (`ZONES`), hero movement / regen / respawn (`HERO_FEEL`), enemy aggro (`AGGRO`: radius, leash, reach), path shape, enemy feel, z layers, ultimates (`ULT`). Hero / enemy / wave numbers live in `src/data/`.
- `src/data/heroes.ts` — hero base stats (damage, interval, range, HP, armor, role) and the body/weapon rig (weapon offset, windup and recover times). `src/data/enemies.ts` — every enemy kind as data (HP, speed, XP, leak, scale, alpha, `attack` on heroes, `flying` = ignores heroes, and traits: `armor` halves arrow damage, `immune` ignores control, `heal` aura, `split` on death, `boss` with fixed HP, `summon`, `revive`), `ELITE` multipliers, per-wave HP growth (bosses don't grow). `src/data/waves.ts` — the 20-wave table (groups of `{ kind, count, interval, delay, elite? }`; wave 10 brings the slime king, wave 20 the lich — killing it wins the run).
- `src/data/skills.ts` — the skill tree as pure data: `BRANCHES` (hero × branch × 4 levels; level 4 is the evolution), each node's `apply(mods)` edits that hero's per-run mods (`ArcherMods`: arrows, damage / crit / range multipliers, poison, pierce, headshot, poison cloud; `MageMods`: damage, blast radius, burning ground, slow, freeze, chain lightning; `KnightMods`: damage, knockback, collide, arc, range, interval, whirl, stun, HP, lifesteal, damage taken, taunt); `availableNodes` (next level of each branch of placed heroes), `GENERIC` options (repeatable; effect + amount) and `RunMods` (run-wide multipliers), `drawOffers` (weighted: nodes 1, generic `GENERIC_WEIGHT`), `xpToNext`.
- `src/path.ts` — `randomPath` (random control points joined with `Curve2D.catmullRom`) and `linePath` for tests.
- `src/assets.ts` — every texture: the `heroes` and `enemies` atlases from the art pipeline (sprites stored at 2×, shown at `ART_SCALE` 0.5, pivots at the feet), the `fx` atlas (black-background effects that must be drawn with `blendMode: 'add'` — put them under Battle's `fx` node — plus the transparent `fx_arrow`), the `ui` atlas (`ui_panel` / `ui_button` nine-slice frames, skill / generic / ultimate icons) and `bg.jpg`. The remaining placeholders from `scripts/gen-placeholders.mjs` are simple shapes (glow, spark, dot, range ring) kept on purpose. The tombstone is `heroes.get('tombstone')`.
- `src/sounds.ts` — every sound effect with its volume and `maxVoices` (hits, deaths and explosions are capped so a big wave doesn't stack dozens of copies), `playSound`, the looping `BGM`, and `SOUND_ASSETS` (spread into Battle's `static assets`). Battle plays them through `battle.sound(name)`; the HUD's top-right toggles mute the Music / SFX buses and save `musicMuted` / `sfxMuted`. Files are in `public/assets/audio/` (CC0, sources in `CREDITS.md`); paths are string literals so the Vite plugin can check them.
- `art/` + `scripts/art/` — the art pipeline (`pnpm art`, see `scripts/art/README.md`): `art/assets.ts` lists every asset (prompt, mode, refs, background, display height, atlas, pivot, `from`; `plain` skips the character style prompt and anchor, `key: false` skips keying for black-background effects and the background, `aspect` / `displayWidth` for the 9:16 background, `publish: 'jpg'` writes a standalone file instead of packing; atlases are palette-quantized PNGs), `art/style.md` is the shared style prompt. Chosen candidates live in `art/refs/` (committed) and are pinned with `from`, so re-keying / re-packing never changes them. Gemini generates into `art/raw/` (gitignored), `key.ts` chroma-keys the background and its shadows, sprites are resized to 2× display height into `art/sprites/` (committed), then packed into `public/assets/<atlas>.png/.json` for `atlas()`. Generation needs `GEMINI_API_KEY`.
- `src/scenes/Battle.ts` — the whole fight: hero choosing and placing (`openHeroPicker` → `choose` → `placeHero` puts the hero in `zoneFor(kind)`; again before waves 3 and 6, from the remaining heroes; `IMPLEMENTED` lists the heroes that can be picked), `nearestEnemy` / `chaseTarget` for hero movement, `_tickAggro` (ground enemies pick the nearest living hero within `AGGRO.radius`, drop it past `AGGRO.leash` or when it dies, taunted ones only target the knight; in reach they attack every `attack.interval`) and `hurtHero`, waves (`startWave`, spawners, gap between waves), leaks and lives, win / loss, `findTarget` / `findTargets` (in range, least remaining path), arrows (homing or piercing), `damage(e, amount, { crit, dot, ignoreArmor })`, status effects ticked here every frame (poison, slow, freeze, stun, taunt, burning ground; tint priority frozen > stun > taunt > slow > poison; `controllable()` is where immunity goes), the knight's `slash` (cone or 360°, knockback via `Enemy.pushBack`, stun roll, collide, lifesteal in `damage`) and `tauntAura` (forces nearby ground enemies onto the knight), bosses (`_tickBosses`: summons from the boss's position on new paths; the lich revives recent `graves` near it; boss entrance and HUD bar), ultimates (energy charged by each hero's own damage via `damage(…, { source })`, `canUlt` = hero alive + full + 12 s since the last + not in a picker; `arrowRain`, `meteor` with `hitStop`, `knightCharge` + `chargeSweep`), XP and levels (`gainXp` × `run.xpMul` → `openPicker` pauses the tree → `applyOffer` → `applySkill` / `applyGeneric`), the end of a run (`_showResult` saves `bestWave` / `wins` and opens `ResultPanel`; `restart`), particles, damage numbers, screen shake.
- `src/nodes/Hero.ts` — abstract `Hero` (walks inside its `zone` toward `moveGoal()`: ranged heroes hold still while something is in range, otherwise step toward the nearest enemy, else go `home`; the knight chases `chaseTarget(zone)`; a hero placed with `at` has no zone and never moves. HP with `takeDamage` / `heal`, armor, out-of-combat regen, death → tombstone + countdown → `respawn()` with brief invulnerability. Body + weapon sprites; the attack is a tween: squash windup → `release()` → stretch → settle; the whole node flips with `scale.x` to face the target) `Archer`, `Mage` and `Knight`. `weaponAngle()` / `weaponPose()` shape each weapon's motion (the knight swings: raised back → swung through). `Hero.stats` is recomputed by `refreshStats()`: base stats → the hero's skill mods (`applyMods`) → run-wide damage and attack speed (`world.run`). `Archer.release` builds a `Shot` per arrow (crit roll, every 5th a headshot, poison, pierce); `Mage.release` casts a fireball with a `Blast` payload and every Nth cast a chain lightning.
- `src/nodes/Enemy.ts` — follows its `Curve2D` (`dist`, `remaining`, `pushBack`); with a `target` it leaves the path (offset `ox` / `oy`, path progress frozen) and stops at `reach`, then walks back to the path when the target is gone; `lunge()` when it attacks; body sprite pivoted at the feet (the HP bar sits on top of its displayed height), hops and squashes while walking, `flash` on hit, overhead HP bar, `zIndex = y`; status fields (poison, slow, freeze, stun, taunt) that Battle ticks; elites are bigger with a gold glow and multiply XP / leak; `heal()` for the shaman.
- `src/nodes/Arrow.ts`, `src/nodes/FloatText.ts` — pooled arrows (a `Shot` payload; piercing arrows fly straight and report every enemy they touch once) and damage numbers. `src/nodes/UpgradePicker.ts` — the level-up cards (`processMode: 'always'` so it works while the tree is paused). `src/nodes/HeroPicker.ts` — the hero cards (with a mini map of the hero's zone). `src/nodes/ResultPanel.ts` — win / loss, wave, kills, time, records and the build summary (`buildSummary`). `src/nodes/Effects.ts` — `Fireball` (pooled), `BurnZone`, `Bolt` (the lightning texture rotated and stretched between two points). `src/nodes/Ultimates.ts` — `UltBar` (three buttons, greyed with a respawn countdown while the hero is dead; archer: hold and drag to aim, drop on the button to cancel; mage: tap, then tap the field; knight: tap) emitting `aimStart` / `aimMove` / `aimEnd` / `cast`, `AimRing`, `RainZone`, `MeteorStrike`. `src/nodes/PathPreview.ts` — dotted route preview that fades out (elites and bosses: the full route in gold; normal enemies: the first `PATH.previewLength` px, fainter, at most one per `PATH.previewGap` s). `src/nodes/ui.ts` — `panelOptions` / `button` for the wood nine-slice frames and the ink colors for text on parchment. `src/nodes/Hud.ts` (the boss bar is small and semi-transparent: it sits over the spawn area).
- `test/sim.ts` + `test/balance.test.ts` — headless full runs with two strategies (novice: random picks, no ultimates; reasonable: knight → mage → archer, evolutions first, ultimates on the densest spot) over seeds 1–9; the balance test checks novice median wave ≥ 8 and mostly losing, reasonable ≥ 6/9 wins with median lives ≤ 12 and 9–15 minute runs. **Run `pnpm balance` after changing any hero, enemy, wave or skill number** to see per-run leaks, deaths and ultimates.
- `test/zones.test.ts` — zones and spawn points, knight chasing and returning, ranged heroes staying in their zone, aggro / leaving and rejoining the path / flying enemies, elite attack, knockback while engaged, regen, death / tombstone / respawn / invulnerability, no ultimate while dead, the generic HP option, the charge returning to where it started.
- `test/audio.test.ts` — background music, sounds for key events (`g.audio.log`), the per-sound cap, and the mute toggles persisting across a restart.
- `test/art.test.ts` — chroma key on a synthetic image (background, its shadow, enclosed background, anti-aliased edges) and the packed atlas read back by `atlas()`.
- `test/bosses.test.ts` — fixed HP and immunity, HUD bar, leak cost, summon cadence, revive radius / window / caps, winning by killing the lich.
- `test/enemies.test.ts` — armor, splitting, shaman heals, ghost immunity, elites and the wave table.
- `test/ultimates.test.ts` — energy and its cap, the 12 s gap, each ultimate's area and damage, and the button / drag / tap controls.
- `test/knight.test.ts` — cone and whirl hits, knockback and collide, stun, the guard branch (HP, lifesteal, damage taken) and taunt.
- `test/mage.test.ts` — choosing heroes (they appear in their zone), unlocks before waves 3 and 6, and every mage branch.
- `test/run.test.ts` — every generic option, offer weights, the build summary, the result screen and saved records.
- `test/skills.test.ts` — level-ups (pause, offers, consecutive levels, maxed branches) and every archer node's effect.
- `test/battle.test.ts` — paths and preview, targeting, hit timing, kills and XP, waves, losing and restarting, winning after 20 waves.

**Don't name subclass fields like engine privates** (`_scale`, `_flash`, `_texture`…): TypeScript rejects a subclass field with the same name as a base class's private one. `Enemy` uses `_sizeScale` for this reason; see `.scratch/engine-ergonomics/`.

**Hero origins are at the feet**: the body sprite is pivoted at the feet, so `hero.position` is where it stands and range checks measure from the feet.

**Tests call `battle.startWith(kind, at?)`** to skip the hero picker (with `at` the hero stands still at that point — most old tests use the old slot positions such as `v(375, 740)`; without it the hero goes to its zone and moves), then `battle.stopSpawning()` to place their own enemies (it also stops waves from advancing).

## Workflow

Same as the other examples: test first with `createTestGame`, `pnpm check`, `pnpm dev` for the browser, `pnpm build:wechat` for the WeChat Mini Game (logs `[perf] …` every 5 s — build with `SAPLING_LOG_URL` and run `pnpm log-server` to read them).
